WORKSPACE UTILITIES

Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Scope and access

Workspace Utilities is a private, owner-operated application. It accesses Google account information and workspace content only through permissions authorized by the account holder. Depending on the configured workflow, this can include files, documents, spreadsheets, email messages and attachments, calendar entries, and task lists, together with associated metadata.

Use of information

Authorized data is used to perform requested organization, analysis, document preparation, synchronization, and communication tasks. The application may read or update authorized resources and send messages when the account holder has specifically authorized the workflow.

Processing and disclosure

Processing may occur on the operator's devices and through Google and configured software or AI service providers as needed for those tasks. Data may be included in a requested output or sent to a recipient only as authorized by the account holder. This application does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Private workspace content and credentials are not published on this website.

Workspace Utilities' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage, security, and deletion

Authorization credentials are stored using the configured local credential protection. Working data, generated outputs, and operational records may be retained locally or in the account holder's authorized workspace services for continuity and review. They are retained until removed by the operator or under the relevant provider's retention controls. Access is limited to authorized workflows and services; no system can guarantee absolute security.

The account holder can revoke access through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized access but does not automatically remove existing local copies or outputs. Those can be deleted separately from the relevant storage locations.

Website visitors and contact

This site has no application analytics, advertising, cookies, or data-entry forms. GitHub Pages may process technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

For questions or a private contact arrangement, use the project contact page without posting account contents, credentials, or other private information. This policy will be updated if the application's data practices change.
